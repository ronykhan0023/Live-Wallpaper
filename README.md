Rony Live Wallpaper — Bring Your Screen to Life

Turn your favorite videos, animated GIFs, and photos into smooth native Android live wallpapers. Built on Android's official WallpaperService and Jetpack Media3 ExoPlayer, Rony Live Wallpaper is optimized for reliable performance from Android 9 up to Android 16.

Key Features:
• Unlimited Videos & Custom Trim/Cut: Use videos of any duration as your live wallpaper or trim/cut the exact clip range you want.
• Videos, Animated GIFs & Photos: Full support for MP4/MKV/WebM videos, animated GIFs, and high-resolution photos in one studio.
• Precision Speed Control (0.25x – 3.00x): Choose quick presets, adjust the fine slider, or type an exact decimal playback speed (such as 0.40x) directly from your keyboard.
• Framing, Zoom & Drag Position: Customize Scale mode (Crop, Fit, Fill, Center), zoom level, screen alignment by dragging, brightness, seamless looping, and audio mute per item.
• Smart Scheduled Playlists: Group photos, GIFs, and videos into playlists that auto-rotate by seconds, minutes, hours, days, or months—with full playlist editing, shuffle mode, and per-item adjustments.
• Quick Settings Tile & Home Widget: Switch playlist wallpapers instantly from the Quick Settings shade or Home Screen widget.
• Battery-Smart & 100% Offline: Automatically pauses playback when the screen is off to save power. All media stays private inside your device storage with zero external uploads.
• Modern UI & Bilingual Support: AMOLED Dark and Light themes with full English and Bengali (বাংলা) language support.
